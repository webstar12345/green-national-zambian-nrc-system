# 📱 NRC Card Download User Guide

## 🎯 How to Download Your NRC Card

Once your NRC application has been approved by an admin, you can download your NRC card in multiple ways. This guide shows you all the available options.

---

## 🔔 Method 1: Home Page Notifications (Recommended)

### Step 1: Login to Your Account
- Go to the login page
- Enter your username and password
- Complete OTP verification (if required)

### Step 2: Check Home Page Alerts
- After logging in, you'll be redirected to the Home page
- Look for **notification alerts** at the top of the page
- You'll see colorful notification banners with your application status

### Step 3: Download from Notifications
- **For "NRC Ready" notifications**: Click the **"Download Now"** button (orange, animated)
- **For "Application Approved" notifications**: Click the **"Download NRC"** button
- This will take you directly to your NRC card page

---

## 📋 Method 2: My Applications Page

### Step 1: Navigate to My Applications
- Click **"My Applications"** in the navigation menu
- Or click **"View My Applications"** button on the home page

### Step 2: Find Your Approved Application
- Look for applications with **green "Approved"** status
- You'll see a green box with "NRC Ready!" message
- Multiple download options are available:
  - **"View Card"** - See your NRC card
  - **"Download"** - Download both sides as ZIP

---

## 📄 Method 3: Application Detail Page

### Step 1: Access Application Details
- From "My Applications", click **"View Details"** on any application
- Or visit directly: `/application/[ID]/`

### Step 2: Use the NRC Banner (For Approved Applications)
- You'll see a **large green banner** at the top saying "🎉 Your NRC Card is Ready!"
- The banner shows your NRC number
- Multiple action buttons are available:
  - **"View NRC Card"** - Preview your card
  - **"Add/Update Signature"** - Manage digital signature

### Step 3: Download Options
- **Individual Downloads**:
  - **"Download Front Side"** - Front of NRC as PNG
  - **"Download Back Side"** - Back of NRC as PNG
  - **"Download Both Sides (ZIP)"** - Both sides in one ZIP file

---

## 🎫 Method 4: Direct NRC Card View

### Step 1: View Your NRC Card
- Click any **"View NRC Card"** button
- Or visit directly: `/application/[ID]/nrc-card/`

### Step 2: Download from Card View
- You'll see your complete NRC card (front and back)
- Download buttons are available:
  - **"Download Front Side"**
  - **"Download Back Side"**  
  - **"Download Both Sides"**

---

## 📱 Available Download Formats

| Format | Description | Use Case |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| **PNG (Front)** | High-quality front side image | Printing, official use |
| **PNG (Back)** | High-quality back side image | Complete documentation |
| **ZIP (Both)** | Both sides in one archive | Backup, sharing |

## 🔍 Troubleshooting

### ❓ "I don't see download options"
**Possible causes:**
- Application not yet approved by admin
- NRC card not yet generated
- You're not logged in

**Solutions:**
1. Check application status in "My Applications"
2. Wait for admin approval
3. Contact admin if approved but no NRC available

### ❓ "Download buttons don't work"
**Solutions:**
1. Refresh the page
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try a different browser
4. Check internet connection

### ❓ "I can't find my application"
**Solutions:**
1. Make sure you're logged into the correct account
2. Check "My Applications" page
3. Contact support with your application details

---

## 📞 Need Help?

If you're still having trouble downloading your NRC card:

1. **Check Notifications**: Look for unread notifications on the home page
2. **Verify Status**: Ensure your application status is "Approved"
3. **Contact Admin**: If approved but no download options available
4. **Technical Support**: For website or download issues

## 🎯 Quick Access URLs

For users with approved applications:

- **Home Page**: `/home/`
- **My Applications**: `/my-applications/`
- **Application Detail**: `/application/[ID]/`
- **NRC Card View**: `/application/[ID]/nrc-card/`
- **Download Front**: `/application/[ID]/download/front/`
- **Download Back**: `/application/[ID]/download/back/`
- **Download Both**: `/application/[ID]/download/both/`

Replace `[ID]` with your application ID number.
