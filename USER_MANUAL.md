# 📖 NRC System User Manual
## Step-by-Step Guide for Citizens

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### What is the NRC System?
The National Registration Card (NRC) System is Zambia's digital platform for applying for and managing your National Registration Card online. No more long queues or paper forms!

### What You Need
- **Email Address** (for account verification)
- **Phone Number** (for contact)
- **Supporting Documents** (birth certificate, proof of residence)
- **Digital Camera/Phone** (to take photos of documents)

---

## 📱 Creating Your Account

### Step 1: Visit the Website
Go to your NRC system website (provided by your local registration office)

### Step 2: Sign Up
1. Click **"Create Account"** or **"Sign Up"**
2. Fill in your details:
   - **Username**: Choose something you'll remember
   - **Email**: Use a valid email you check regularly
   - **Password**: At least 8 characters, mix of letters and numbers
   - **Full Name**: As it should appear on your NRC

### Step 3: Verify Your Email
1. Check your email inbox (and spam folder)
2. You'll receive a 6-digit code
3. Enter this code on the verification page
4. Your account is now active!

---

## 🔐 Logging In

### Regular Login
1. Enter your **username** and **password**
2. Check your email for a 6-digit security code
3. Enter the code to complete login
4. You're now in your dashboard!

### Google Login (Alternative)
1. Click **"Sign in with Google"**
2. Choose your Google account
3. Complete email verification if required

## 📝 Applying for Your NRC

### New NRC Application

#### Personal Information
Fill in these details carefully:
- **Full Names**: Exactly as they should appear on your NRC
- **Date of Birth**: Use the format DD/MM/YYYY
- **Gender**: Male or Female
- **Nationality**: Zambian (for citizens)

#### Address Information
Provide your current residential address:
- **Province**: Select from dropdown
- **District**: Choose your district
- **Constituency**: Your voting constituency
- **Ward**: Local ward
- **Village/Area**: Specific location name

#### Contact Information
- **Phone Number**: Include country code (+260)
- **Email Address**: Your verified email
- **Alternative Contact**: Emergency contact person

#### Document Upload
You need to upload clear photos of:
1. **Birth Certificate**
   - Take a clear photo in good lighting
   - Ensure all text is readable
   - File size should be under 5MB

2. **Proof of Residence**
   - Utility bill, rental agreement, or affidavit
   - Must show your current address
   - Document should be recent (within 3 months)

3. **Passport Photo**
   - Recent color photo
   - Plain background (white or light blue)
   - Face clearly visible, no sunglasses or hat

#### Digital Signature
1. Use the signature pad on screen
2. Sign with your finger or mouse
3. Make sure it's clear and matches your usual signature
4. You can clear and redo if needed

### Replacement NRC Application

If you need to replace a lost, damaged, or outdated NRC:

1. Select **"Replacement NRC"**
2. Choose reason:
   - **Lost**: NRC was lost or stolen
   - **Damaged**: NRC is torn, faded, or damaged
   - **Name Change**: After marriage or legal name change
   - **Other**: Specify the reason

3. Provide previous NRC details (if available):
   - Previous NRC number
   - Date it was issued
   - Where it was issued

4. Complete all other steps as for new application

## 📊 Tracking Your Application

### Dashboard Overview
Your main dashboard shows:
- **Application Status**: Pending, Under Review, Approved, or Rejected
- **Submission Date**: When you applied
- **Expected Processing Time**: Estimated completion
- **Recent Updates**: Any changes or requests

### Application Details
Click on any application to see:
- **Complete application information**
- **Uploaded documents**
- **Processing timeline**
- **Admin comments** (if any)
- **Next steps** (if action required)

### Status Meanings
- **Pending**: Application received, waiting for review
- **Under Review**: Being processed by registration officer
- **Additional Info Required**: You need to provide more documents
- **Approved**: NRC approved, ready for collection/delivery
- **Rejected**: Application denied (reason provided)

## 📋 Document Requirements Checklist

### For New NRC
✅ **Birth Certificate** (original or certified copy)  
✅ **Proof of Residence** (utility bill, rental agreement)  
✅ **Passport Photo** (recent, color, plain background)  
✅ **Parent's NRC** (if applicant is under 18)  
✅ **Marriage Certificate** (if applicable)  

### For Replacement NRC
✅ **Police Report** (if lost or stolen)  
✅ **Affidavit** (sworn statement of loss)  
✅ **Proof of Residence** (current address)  
✅ **Passport Photo** (recent)  
✅ **Previous NRC** (if damaged, not lost)  

### Document Quality Requirements
- **Clear and Readable**: All text must be visible
- **Good Lighting**: No shadows or glare
- **Correct Orientation**: Right way up, not sideways
- **Full Document**: Show entire document, not cropped
- **Recent**: Documents should be current (within 3 months for proof of residence)

## 🎯 Tips for Success

### Before You Start
1. **Gather Documents**: Collect all required documents first
2. **Good Photos**: Ensure you can take clear photos
3. **Stable Internet**: Use reliable internet connection
4. **Quiet Time**: Set aside 30-45 minutes without interruptions

### During Application
1. **Double-Check**: Review all information before submitting
2. **Clear Photos**: Retake blurry or unclear document photos
3. **Save Progress**: The system saves your progress automatically
4. **Contact Info**: Ensure your phone and email are correct

### After Submission
1. **Keep Reference**: Note your application reference number
2. **Check Email**: Monitor for updates and requests
3. **Be Patient**: Allow full processing time before inquiring
4. **Respond Quickly**: If additional info is requested, provide it promptly
